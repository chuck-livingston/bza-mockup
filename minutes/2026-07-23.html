Format
Hybrid — Council Chamber, Room 115, City Hall, 401 N. Morton Street, Bloomington, IN 47404, and via Zoom
Members present
Flavia Burrell, Leslie Kutsenkow, and Jo Throckmorton in Council Chambers; Chris Smith via Zoom

Approval of minutes

June 25, 2026 minutes. Burrell moved to approve; Kutsenkow seconded. Passed 3:1 (roll call; Smith abstained).

Reports, resolutions, and communications

Eric Greulich, Development Services Manager, reported that an adjacent property owner did not observe a Notice of Public Hearing yard sign for case ZR2026-06-0025 posted within the 21-day rule. The petitioner submitted a signed affidavit that the Notice of Public Hearing letter was mailed to nearby property owners.

Leo Pilachowski, an adjacent property owner, stated he did not want to request a continuance of this petition but wanted to ensure his presence did not waive any possible defect in notice. His presence was to request a change of conditions.

Petitions continued to a future meeting

  • ZR2026-07-0033 — 530 South Washington Street. Administrative appeal of a Notice of Violation warning letter for a sign in the public right-of-way, a prohibited sign location, by Joseph Davis. (Case manager: Gabriel Holbrow)
  • ZR2026-05-0022 and ZR2026-07-0035 — 412 E. Wylie Street. Conditional use approval for a duplex dwelling in the Residential Small Lot (R3) zoning district, plus variances from maximum vehicle parking allowance number and vehicle parking location/design standards, by Kevin Spicer. (Case manager: Jamie Kreindler)

Expedited agenda

  1. ZR2026-06-0032 — 2101 E. Covenanter Drive and 1213 S. High Street

    Conditional use approval to allow development (transportation facilities) within the Floodway and Floodway Fringe for the High Street multiuse path project, in a Residential Medium Lot (R2) zoning district, by the City of Bloomington Engineering Department.

    Case manager: Joe Patterson.

    Staff recommended approval with the following condition:

    1. Floodplain Development Permit and Site Development Permit approvals are required prior to work commencing.

    Neil Kopper (petitioner) presented.

    Public comment

    • Jennifer Long

    Motion: Kutsenkow moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact and approve; Burrell seconded.

    Passed 4:0 (roll call), with staff's recommended condition.

  2. ZR2026-06-0029 — 120 S. Walnut Street

    Variance from sign standards to allow an off-premise sign not required to be on a tenant's space, for a "financial institution" use in the Mixed-Use Downtown Courthouse Square (MD-CS) zoning district, by Fastsigns.

    Case manager: Jamie Kreindler.

    Staff recommended approval with the following condition:

    1. This approval allows this tenant, and future users of this tenant space, to place 13 square feet of wall signage at this location on the building.

    Leighla Taylor (petitioner) presented.

    Motion: Burrell moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact and approve; Burrell seconded.

    Passed 4:0 (roll call), with staff's recommended condition.

Petitions

  1. ZR2026-06-0025 — 716 S. High Street

    Variances from driveway width standards and impervious surface coverage for a single-family detached dwelling in the Residential Medium Lot (R2) zoning district, by Carly Swift.

    Case manager: Jamie Kreindler.

    Staff recommended approval with the following conditions:

    1. Parking area limited to 20' by 20' to accommodate a maximum of two parking spaces.
    2. Six shrubs required along the east side of the parking area, parallel to S. High Street.

    Carly Swift (petitioner) presented.

    Public comment

    • Leo Pilachoski

    Kutsenkow first moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact and approve with staff's recommended conditions; the motion received no second and failed.

    Motion: Smith moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact and approve the requested variances with amended conditions — parking area limited to 360 square feet, a maximum of 2 spaces, and a maximum width of 20'. Kutsenkow seconded.

    Passed 3:1 (roll call; Throckmorton dissented).

  2. ZR2026-06-0027 — 1219 W East Branch Road

    Variances from required sidewalk installation and the build-to line requirement, for a single-family detached dwelling in the Residential Small Lot (R3) zoning district, by Ernest Xi (Valu-Built Construction LLC).

    Case manager: Jamie Kreindler.

    Staff recommended approving the build-to line variance and denying the sidewalk-installation variance, with the following conditions:

    1. Unless approved through the payment-in-lieu process, a 6' wide concrete sidewalk is required along both property frontages.
    2. Street trees not more than 30' from center are required along both property frontages, per UDO requirements.

    Sable Beyers presented on behalf of the petitioner.

    Public comment

    • Pamela Hurt

    Motion: Burrell moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact, approve the build-to line variance, and deny the sidewalk- installation variance, with staff's recommended conditions. Kutsenkow seconded.

    Passed 4:0 (roll call).

  3. ZR2026-06-0030 — 3430 West 3rd Street

    Variance from sign standards applying to drive-through uses in the Mixed-Use Corridor (MC) zoning district, by Crew Carwash.

    Case manager: Gabriel Holbrow.

    Staff recommended denying the requested variance.

    Christina Blackwell (consultant) presented on behalf of the petitioner.

    Motion: Burrell moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact and deny the requested variance; Smith seconded.

    Denied, 3:1 (roll call; Kutsenkow dissented).

Meeting adjourned at 7:54 p.m.
