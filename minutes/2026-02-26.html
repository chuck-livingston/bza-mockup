Board of Zoning Appeals — Meeting Minutes

February 26, 2026 at 5:30 p.m.

Format
Hybrid — Council Chamber, Room 115, City Hall, 401 N. Morton Street, Bloomington, IN 47404, and via Zoom
Members present
Tim Ballard, Flavia Burrell, John Fernandez, Jo Throckmorton

Approval of minutes

January 22, 2026 minutes. Burrell moved to approve; Ballard seconded. Passed 4:0 (roll call).

Old business

Fernandez moved to hear an item of old business staff wished to address; Ballard seconded. Passed 4:0.

Margie Rice, Corporation Counsel, briefed the Board on a lawsuit filed against the BZA by Lamar Advertising on October 18, 2022, and requested the Board delegate authority to the BZA chair to execute a settlement agreement.

Fernandez moved to delegate the BZA chair to execute the settlement agreement; Burrell seconded. Passed 4:0.

Reports, resolutions, and communications

Eric Greulich, Development Services Manager, reported the department is working on an expedited-agenda implementation: petitions where staff recommends approval would be placed on a "Consent Agenda." This would require adjusting the BZA Rules and Procedures, planned to come before the Board at the March 26, 2026 meeting.

Petitions continued to March 26, 2026

  • ZR2026-01-0001 — 618 W. Howe Street. Variance from setback standards and maximum impervious surface coverage to allow an addition to a single-family dwelling in the Residential Small-Lot (R3) zoning district, by Michael Burnett and Heidi Beidinger-Burnett. (Case manager: David Brantez)

Petitions

  1. A. ZR2025-12-0114 — 2511 N. Dunn Street

    Variances from minimum lot area, minimum lot width, minimum side setbacks, tree and forest preservation standards, and riparian buffer requirements to allow an 18-lot single-family subdivision in the Residential Medium Lot (R2) zoning district, by Paul Pruitt and Keith Kline.

    Case manager: Keith Kline. Presented by Jamie Kreindler.

    Staff recommended approving the variances from tree canopy preservation, tree protection fencing, and riparian buffer intermediate/fringe zone disturbance activities, but denying the variances from minimum lot area, minimum lot width, and minimum side building setback, with conditions including:

    1. Subject to terms and conditions of Plan Commission Case #SUB2025-12-0051.
    2. Petitioner responsible for replacing any improperly protected trees damaged during construction.
    3. Continued work with staff on a replanting plan to remediate canopy loss.
    4. Trees required along the north and south property lines as shown.
    5. Staking of streamside zones required in the riparian area.
    6. Sidewalk within the designated riparian area to be constructed with permeable pavers.
    7. Final acceptance and approval from City of Bloomington Utilities (CBU) required.
    8. Variances apply only to the general lot/street layout as submitted; substantially different plans must meet all UDO standards.

    Paul Pruitt (petitioner) and Daniel Butler (project engineer) presented.

    Public comment

    • Jessica Will
    • Julia Livingston
    • Hopi Stosberg (via Zoom)
    • Jim Hart
    • Tom Yeiser
    • Robin Young
    • Marlyn Bauchat
    • Greg Grant
    • Stephanie Dickinson
    • Susan Sandberg
    • Meghan Blair

    Motion: Fernandez moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact and approve the variances from tree canopy preservation, tree protection fencing, and riparian buffer disturbance activities, but deny the variances from minimum lot area, minimum lot width, and minimum side building setback. Ballard seconded.

    Passed 4:0 (roll call), with staff-recommended conditions of approval.

  2. B. ZR2025-12-0115 — 614 N. Grant Street

    Variance from tree and forest preservation standards to allow tree removal for construction of a new triplex dwelling in the Residential High-Density Multifamily (RH) zoning district, by Valu-Built, Inc.

    Case manager: Gabriel Holbrow.

    Staff recommended approval with the following condition:

    1. At least nine new trees, from species listed in the UDO as permitted large street or interior trees, must be planted in addition to required street trees, verified by a staff site inspection before or within six weeks of unit occupancy.

    Ernest Xi (petitioner) presented.

    Public comment

    • Amy Butler
    • James Ford

    Motion: Fernandez moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact and approve; Burrell seconded.

    Passed 4:0 (roll call), with staff's recommended condition.

  3. C. ZR2025-12-0116 — 1004 S. Henderson Street

    Conditional use approval to allow a duplex dwelling in the Residential Small Lot (R3) zoning district, by H&E Rentals, LLC.

    Case manager: Joe Patterson.

    Staff recommended approval with the following conditions:

    1. Conditional use approval limited to the design shown and discussed in the packet.
    2. Petitioner must pass a HAND rental inspection and procure a rental permit for any rented units.

    Eric Masten (petitioner) presented.

    Public comment

    • David Stewart
    • Jon Lawrence
    • Cory Hawkins
    • Jan Sorby

    Motion: Ballard moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact and approve; Fernandez seconded.

    Passed 4:0 (roll call), with an added condition:

    3. Petitioner shall work with staff to avoid requiring back-out parking.

  4. D. ZR2025-12-0117 — 105 E. Driscoll Drive

    Conditional use approval to allow a duplex dwelling in the Residential Small-Lot (R3) zoning district, by Rusty Wampler.

    Case manager: David Brantez.

    Public comment
