Petitions

  1. ZR2026-05-0020 — 1300 S. Patterson Drive

    Variance from sign standards to allow ten freestanding signs for a "manufacturing, light" use in a Planned Unit Development (PUD) zoning district, by Novo Nordisk.

    Case manager: Eric Greulich.

    Staff recommended approval with the following conditions:

    1. Variance applies to the signs as proposed only; new signage must meet UDO requirements.
    2. All signs along public roads (Signs #7, 13, 11, 10, 5, and 4) must be landscaped per UDO requirements.
    3. Sign #2, adjacent to the parking area along Rogers Street, must be no taller than 6'.

    Kenton Pardue (consultant) and Brian Rayles (petitioner) presented.

    Motion: Fernandez moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact and approve; Kutsenkow seconded.

    Passed 3:0 (roll call), with staff's recommended conditions.

  2. ZR2026-05-0022 — 412 E. Wylie Street

    Conditional use approval to allow a duplex dwelling in the Residential Small Lot (R3) zoning district, by Kevin Spicer.

    Case manager: Jamie Kreindler.

    Staff recommended approval with the following conditions:

    1. Conditional use approval limited to the design shown and discussed in the packet.
    2. A right-of-way use permit is required prior to any work in the public right-of-way.

  3. ZR2026-05-0023 — 1155 S. College Mall Road

    Variance from maximum wall signage allowance for a multi-tenant restaurant use in the Mixed-Use Corridor (MC) zoning district, by Lisa Rains (Sign Solutions Inc.).

    Case manager: Jamie Kreindler.

    Staff recommended denying the petition.

    Thomas Hopkins (petitioner) presented on behalf of Crumble.

    Motion: Fernandez moved to adopt Alternative Findings of Fact and approve, with an added condition that the submitted sign renderings not be modified. Kutsenkow seconded.

    Passed 3:0 (roll call).

    Alternative findings of fact #2 and #3

    1. Adjacent use and value: no substantially adverse impacts to the use or value of surrounding properties are found as a result of the variance.
    2. Practical difficulties: strict application of the UDO would result in practical difficulties given the building's elevation and landscaping along the College Mall Road frontage, which would make UDO-compliant signage ineffective.

Meeting adjourned at 7:48 p.m.
