March 26, 2026 at 5:30 p.m. (draft)

Format
Hybrid — Council Chamber, Room 115, City Hall, 401 N. Morton Street, Bloomington, IN 47404, and via Zoom
Members present
Tim Ballard, Flavia Burrell, Jo Throckmorton

Approval of minutes

February 26, 2026 minutes. Burrell moved to approve; Ballard seconded. Passed 4:0 (roll call).

Reports, resolutions, and communications

Eric Greulich, Development Services Manager, reported that petition ZR2026-02-0004 was requested to be continued to the April 23, 2026 meeting. Burrell moved to continue it; Ballard seconded. Passed 3:0 (roll call).

Greulich explained that, due to accessibility standards, the preparation of the packet and agenda will now follow a different format going forward. He also gave an update on the expedited agenda, which is planned to come before the Board for a vote at the April 23, 2026 meeting.

Petitions continued to April 23, 2026

  • ZR2026-02-0004 — 508 N. Lincoln Street. Variance from front building setback requirements for a deck, for a single-family detached dwelling in the Residential Multifamily (RM) zoning district, by The Brawley Group / Tina Somes. (Case manager: Jamie Kreindler)

Petitions

  1. ZR2026-01-0001 — 618 W. Howe Street

    Variance from setback standards and maximum impervious surface coverage to allow construction of an addition to a single-family detached dwelling in the Residential Small-Lot (R3) zoning district, by Michael Burnett and Heidi Beidinger-Burnett.

    Case manager: David Brantez.

    Staff recommended approval with the following condition:

    1. This variance applies only to the scope of work submitted with this petition; any future additions must meet all UDO requirements.

    Kevin Potter (structural engineer) presented on behalf of the petitioner.

    Motion: Burrell moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact and approve; Ballard seconded.

    Passed 3:0 (roll call), with staff's recommended condition.

  2. ZR2026-02-0006 — 1205 S. Rogers Street

    Administrative appeal of a staff determination regarding decorative stone, by Cream & Crimson Management.

    Case manager: David Brantez. Per UDO Section 20.06.080(d)(3)(B)(ii), staff makes no formal recommendation on administrative appeals.

    Josh Alley (petitioner) presented.

    Motion: Ballard moved to approve the appeal; Burrell seconded.

    Passed 3:0 (roll call).

  3. ZR2026-02-0005 — 1205 S. Rogers Street

    Variance from maximum impervious surface coverage, minimum landscape area, and groundcover standards in the Residential Multifamily (RM) zoning district, by Cream & Crimson Management.

    Case manager: David Brantez.

    Staff recommended denying all three requested variances.

    Josh Alley presented the variance request.

    Public comment

    • Casey Green

    Motion: Burrell moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact and deny; Ballard seconded.

    Motion to deny passed 3:0 (roll call) — the variances were not granted.

  4. ZR2026-03-0008 — 1320 S. Rogers Street

    Conditional use approval to allow a driveway and structures within the floodplain for construction of five buildings for a "Contractor's Yard" use, within Parcel C of the Thomson Planned Unit Development (PUD) zoning district, by Built, LLC.

    Case manager: Eric Greulich.

    Staff recommended approval with the following conditions:

    1. Compliance with all state and federal requirements prior to final occupancy.
    2. A Floodplain Development Permit and Site Development Permit are required prior to any site disturbance.

    Dillon Reynolds (consultant) presented on behalf of the petitioner.

    Motion: Ballard moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact and approve; Burrell seconded.

    Passed 3:0 (roll call), with the staff-recommended conditions.

  5. ZR2026-03-0009 — 302 S. College Ave, 327 S. College Ave, 302 S. Walnut St

    Variance from sign standards for a conference or convention center use, in the Mixed-Use Downtown (MD) Downtown Core (DC) and Downtown Edges Overlay (DEO) zoning districts, by Monroe County Capital Improvement Board / James Witlach.

    Case manager: Jackie Scanlan.

    Staff recommended approval with the following condition:

    1. The freestanding (monument) sign shall not exceed six feet in height; any digital portion must not exceed the same proportion relative to the new height as shown in the application packet.

    Sarah Hempstead (consultant) presented on behalf of the petitioner.

    Public comment

    • Randy Cassady

    Motion: Burrell moved to adopt the Proposed Findings of Fact and approve; Ballard seconded.

    Passed 3:0 (roll call), with staff's recommended condition.

Meeting adjourned at 10:15 p.m.
