Criteria and Findings for Conditional Use Permit

20.06.040(d)(6)(B) — General Compliance Criteria

Proposed finding: the petition meets the applicable use-specific standards. No Notices of Violation are on file, occupancy of each unit is subject to the UDO's "Family" definition, each duplex unit has a separate exterior entrance, the four design elements match the block face, bedroom count and the 150' buffer requirement are both met, and the required neighborhood meeting was held on May 7, 2026. There are no other known applicable regulations. Water and sanitary service will be coordinated with City of Bloomington Utilities, electrical service with Duke Energy; adequate capacity exists, and there are no known prior land-use approvals for this site.

Slide 14 of 33

20.06.040(d)(6)(C)(i) — Consistency with the Comprehensive Plan

Proposed finding: the site's Future Land Use Map designation is "Mixed Urban Residential" — older neighborhoods built on a traditional block-and-grid pattern. Comprehensive Plan Policy 5.3.1 encourages infill and redevelopment with increased density and underutilized housing types such as duplexes; this proposal advances that goal.

Slide 15 of 33

20.06.040(d)(6)(C)(ii) — Adequate public services and facilities

Proposed finding: City of Bloomington Utilities confirms adequate water and sewer capacity. New concrete walkways connect the duplex to E. Wylie Street as required by UDO Section 20.04.050(d)(4)(C). The revised parking arrangement removes the E. Wylie Street drivecut in favor of alley access from the improved stub of S. Palmer Avenue.

Slide 16 of 33

20.06.040(d)(6)(C)(iii) — Minimizes or mitigates adverse impacts

Proposed finding: no regulated natural or scenic features are affected; the site has no closed canopy and is not a historic site. No significant adverse impacts are expected. Following the May 7, 2026 neighborhood meeting, the petitioner added a proposed 6' opaque privacy fence along the east property line — carried forward as a condition of approval.

Recommendation

Staff recommends the BZA:

Adopt the proposed findings, approve the Conditional Use request, and deny both variance requests, subject to the following conditions:

  1. The conditional use approval is limited to the design shown and discussed in the packet.
  2. A right-of-way use permit is required prior to any work in the public right-of-way.
  3. Per the submitted site plan, a 6' tall opaque fence is required along the east property line.
