Why this is a PDF, not fully rebuilt as HTML

Architectural drawings carry precise dimensions, schedules, and scale relationships that are risky to hand-transcribe into HTML — a small transcription error in a measurement could matter in a way it wouldn't in prose. The original PDF is hosted directly below; the summary beneath it describes what each sheet contains for anyone who wants an overview before opening the full drawings.

What's on each sheet

Page 1 — Cover sheet and front/back elevations

Lists the sheet schedule (floor plans on page 2, foundation and roof plans on page 3, elevations on page 4) alongside front and back elevation drawings showing the duplex's two-story massing, matching gable roofline, and a full-width front porch.

Page 2 — Main and 2nd floor plans

Each unit's main floor includes a living area, kitchen, pantry, laundry/ utility room, a bathroom, and a master bedroom with a 2x6 wall. The sheet lists 1,920 square feet for the main floor living area and 1,076 square feet for the 2nd floor. The 2nd floor adds two more bedrooms and a second bathroom per unit. A door schedule and window schedule on this sheet list every opening by size and type (for example, six 3050-2 DH mulled-unit windows and eight hinged interior doors per floor).

Page 3 — 3D kitchen view, roof plan, and foundation plan

Includes a rendered 3D view of a kitchen for reference, a roof plan at 1/8" scale, and a foundation plan at 1/4" scale showing a 34'-8" by 4'-4" porch foundation and bearing wall locations.

Page 4 — Front, back, right, and left elevations

Full elevation drawings for all four sides of the building at 1/4" scale, showing siding, shake siding accents, and trim board placement, plus floor and ceiling height callouts (main floor, 2nd floor, and ridge top height).
